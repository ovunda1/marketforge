Conventions and assumptions

ItemValue
annualizationarithmetic: mean per-period return x periods; volatility x sqrt(periods)
benchmarkSPY (total return)
dsrPSR against the expected maximum Sharpe of dsr_trials zero-skill trials, null variance 1/(T-1)
episodesa run of bars a symbol is held with one sign; trade costs go to the episode they open or close (a flip splits them by size); financing is not allocated
periods_per_year252
returnsP&L over the fixed starting capital (no compounding)
risk_free{"daily_rate": "bp / (3_600_000 - 91 bp) per calendar day, simple", "first": "2006-12-01", "known_at": "last observation on or before the previous bar, carried forward, never back-filled", "last": "2024-12-31", "series": "DTB3 (3-month T-bill, discount basis)", "source": "FRED DTB3 (data/normalized/fred-dtb3/rates.parquet)"}
sharpemean excess return over the sample std of excess returns, x sqrt(periods)
sharpe_seiid: sqrt((1 + SR^2/2)/T); mertens: with sample skew and kurtosis; no autocorrelation correction
sortinomean excess return over sqrt(mean(min(excess, 0)^2)), all periods
stdsample (ddof=1)
